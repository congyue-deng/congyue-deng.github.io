I am a Tayebati postdoctoral fellow at the Schwarzman College of Computing at MIT, working with William T. Freeman and Marin Soljačić. I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Stanford University, advised by Leonidas Guibas. I also had the pleasure of collaborating with Jitendra Malik (UC Berkeley), Kostas Daniilidis (UPenn), and Jeannette Bohg (Stanford). I obtained my B.S. in Mathematics from Tsinghua University in 2020 with a GPA ranking 1/114.

My research interests include 3D computer vision, geometric deep learning, and physical representation learning. In the short term, I aim to integrate geometric representation learning into generative models to better capture the low-dimensional manifold structure of data distributions, with a particular focus on applications in physics and robotics. In the long term, I seek to uncover geometric structures from data to inform the design of neural networks.
